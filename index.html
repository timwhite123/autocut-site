Common questions

Does my video get uploaded?

No. Your video stays on your iPhone. If you turn on AI editing, only the audio of your take (plus the teleprompter script, if you recorded with it) is sent to find the best cut, and it's deleted as soon as the edit is ready. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then AutoCut. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?

Open the menu in AutoCut, tap your account, then Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In AutoCut, open the menu, tap your account, then Delete account. This removes everything AutoCut stores on its servers. Projects on your iPhone stay there until you delete them or the app.

Can I turn AI editing off?

Yes. Open the menu, tap your account, then Turn off AI editing. AutoCut then edits on your iPhone only.
